# The papers this folder kept for reference

The PDF files of the papers this folder kept for reference are not held in the repository. The private literature base holds them in its folder `sources/pdf/bellman-application-papers/`, under the names in the table.

| Paper | Edition held | Published version | Name in the private literature base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jess Benhabib, Alberto Bisin and Mi Luo (2015), "Wealth Distribution and Social Mobility in the US: A Quantitative Approach" | NBER Working Paper 21721, November 2015; 36 pages | American Economic Review 109(5): 1623-1647, May 2019, doi:10.1257/aer.20151684 | `benhabib-2015-wealth-distribution-social-mobility-us.pdf` |

The edition held is the working paper of 2015, not the published article of 2019, although the folder held it until 14 November 2026 as `benhabib-bisin-luo-2019.pdf`, a name that gives the year of publication. Its Table 1 and Table 2 report earnings and estimates other than those of the published article, as `economics.md` records.
