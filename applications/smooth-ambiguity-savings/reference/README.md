# The papers this folder kept for reference

The PDF files of the papers this folder kept for reference are not held in the repository. The private literature base holds them in its folder `sources/pdf/bellman-application-papers/`, under the names in the table.

| Paper | Edition held | Published version | Name in the private literature base |
|---|---|---|---|
| David Backus, Axelle Ferriere and Stanley Zin (2014), "Risk and Ambiguity in Models of Business Cycles" | NBER Working Paper 20319, July 2014; 48 pages | Journal of Monetary Economics 69: 42-63, January 2015, doi:10.1016/j.jmoneco.2014.12.005 | `backus-2014-risk-ambiguity-models-business-cycles.pdf` |
| Nengjiu Ju and Jianjun Miao (2009), "Ambiguity, Learning, and Asset Returns" | MPRA Paper No. 14737, April 2009; 36 pages | Econometrica 80(2): 559-591, 2012, doi:10.3982/ECTA7618 | `ju-miao-2009-ambiguity-learning-asset-returns.pdf` |
| Peter Klibanoff, Massimo Marinacci and Sujoy Mukerji (2005), "A Smooth Model of Decision Making under Ambiguity" | The published article, Econometrica 73(6): 1849-1892, November 2005; 44 pages | The edition held | `klibanoff-2005-smooth-model-decision-making-ambiguity.pdf` |

Two of the editions held are working papers, not the published articles, although the folder held them until 14 November 2026 under names that give the year of publication: the paper of Backus, Ferriere and Zin of 2014 as `backus-ferriere-zin-2015.pdf`, and the paper of Ju and Miao of 2009 as `ju-miao-2012.pdf`. A working paper and the published article can differ in their numbering and in their values. The folder held the third paper as `klibanoff-marinacci-mukerji-2005.pdf`.
